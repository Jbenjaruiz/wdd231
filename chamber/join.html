NP Membership Benefits (No Fee)

  • Basic listing in the online Member Directory.
  • Access to select Chamber newsletters.
  • Invitation to one free networking event per year.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Enhanced listing in the online Member Directory.
  • Subscription to all Chamber newsletters and publications.
  • Discounted rates for Chamber events and workshops.
  • Access to member-only online resources.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze benefits, plus:
  • Eligibility for Member Spotlight on the Chamber homepage.
  • Opportunity to host one "Business After Hours" networking event.
  • Company name listed in annual Chamber report.
  • Two complimentary tickets to the Annual Chamber Gala.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • All Silver benefits, plus:
  • Premium placement in the online Member Directory.
  • Logo featured on Chamber website homepage footer.
  • Opportunity to sponsor a major Chamber event with recognition.
  • Exclusive invitations to VIP receptions with community leaders.
  • One dedicated e-blast to Chamber membership per year.
